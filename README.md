### Project Description
How Many Spikes in Your Window?" is an interactive web app that analyzes uploaded images to count vertical bars in window frames using edge detection. It gamifies this completely unnecessary task by assigning users a Window Score and Personality, while persistently tracking stats, history, and achievements.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Have you ever stared blankly out of a window and found yourself plagued by the sudden, inescapable need to know exactly how many vertical spikes are in the security grill? We thought so. Until now, humanity has been forced to manually count these window bars like peasants, risking miscounts, boredom, and severe eye strain. 

This project solves the crucial, completely unnecessary, and wholly made-up problem of not knowing your window's exact spike count or its hidden personality, bringing cutting-edge image analysis to a task absolutely nobody asked for.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We're fighting this imaginary crisis with the raw, unapologetic power of client-side JavaScript! 
When you upload an image, our highly specialized, definitely-not-over-engineered edge detection algorithm meticulously scans your photo to identify vertical lines. We then crunch these vital numbers to calculate your window's precise spike count, judge your window's inner "personality," and update a completely arbitrary Window Score. Best of all? It's all saved locally in your browser, allowing you to build an impressive resume of useless window analytics, track your historical stats, and unlock achievements that you can brag about to absolutely no one!
